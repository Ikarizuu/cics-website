01 — About the College

Technology education with purpose.

The College of Information and Computer Studies (CICS) is a leader in technological education, committed to academic excellence, innovation, and the highest ethical standards.

Grounded in Lasallian values, CICS fosters a dynamic, inclusive environment where students, faculty, and industry partners collaborate on technological challenges. It aims to inspire and empower future IT leaders and shape a better global digital landscape.
